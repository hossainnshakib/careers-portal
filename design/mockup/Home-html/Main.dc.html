Frequently asked questions

Do I need an account?+
No. Open a role, complete the application and submit it. You will get a reference number.
Which files can I upload?+
PDF, DOC or DOCX for your CV, up to 5 MB. Some roles also ask for a portfolio sample.
Can I write in Bengali?+
Yes. You can write your answers in Bengali or English.
Can I apply to more than one role?+
Yes. Submit a separate application for each role.
How is my data used?+
Only the internal hiring team can see your application. Read the privacy notice for details.
